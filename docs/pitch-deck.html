The problem

The stakeholder who never files a ticket

💬

Complaints are easy

Anyone can scrape a review and list the top gripes. That's a summarizer — and the brief says it scores poorly.

🔍

Latent needs are hard

The pain a user feels but never asks to fix. They describe a workaround — or they churn silently. That's the value.

The insight that wins

Latent ≠ frequent. We measure latency.

Surface complaint

High pain, and everyone names the fix. Cluster & count → the biggest blob wins. The trap.

Latent need

High pain, but users rarely name the fix. This is the silent stakeholder.

latency = pain × (1 − explicit_ask)
